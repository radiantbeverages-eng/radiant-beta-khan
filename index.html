💾 Backup & Restore
ℹ️ About your data:
Your sales data is saved in your browser's local storage. It is not deleted when you clear browsing history or cache.

⚠️ Data is only lost if you tap "Clear Site Data" or "Clear All Data" in your browser settings.

💡 Tip: Add this app to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iOS, or Install App on Android) for the most reliable storage.
Export Backup
Saves all your entries, distributors, raw material stock, and settings as a .json file to your device.
Restore
Restore from Backup
Select a previously exported .json backup file to restore all data. Current data will be replaced.
Scheduled Backups
Radiant automatically saves a full snapshot of your data every midnight — to this device and to the cloud — so you always have a recent copy to fall back on. Snapshots older than 7 days are deleted automatically to save space.
Storage Usage
Tap below to check your current Firestore usage.
Google Drive Auto-Backup
Connect your Google account to automatically save a backup to your Google Drive at your configured backup time. Keeps the last 30 days. Free, no extra accounts needed.
Sync Diagnostic Log
A record of every sync attempt on this device and what happened. If data ever looks wrong, check here first — this shows exactly what the app saw, instead of guessing.
Backup Settings
Controls both the Firestore snapshot schedule and the Google Drive backup time (when connected).
Whichever limit is reached first — days or count — is what applies. Only a recent window of ~30 snapshots is ever cached on this device to protect browser storage; the full history you set here lives in the cloud and is fetched when you open Backup History.
